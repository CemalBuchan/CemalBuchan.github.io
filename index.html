02About

I'm a third-year Game Development student at Business College Helsinki, with 4+ years of hands-on Unity and C# experience. My focus is gameplay programming — gameplay systems, post-processing, animation, and event-driven architecture — but I'm comfortable across the wider development pipeline, including engine-less development in pure C# and Raylib. I'm a Taitaja 2026 Finalist in the Game Development category, Finland's largest vocational skills competition.

I hold a gold-level certificate (30 ECTS) from XAMK Open University's Introduction to Video Games Creation program, along with two additional Unity/C# courses covering TPS development and game programming fundamentals. Alongside my studies, I've spent 3 years working at a volunteer association, taking part in weekly group discussions and developing strong active listening, facilitation, and collaborative problem-solving skills.

I'm a native Turkish speaker, comfortable in English (B2), and currently building my Finnish (A2). Beyond Unity & C#, I've also worked with Raylib, Python, HTML, and CSS.
